## LinkedIn post

People are using AI agents to investigate compiled software and build new implementations from what they learn.

I wanted to see the actual results, so I started REA Casebook.

The first 11 cases include:
• Tracing Notion's clipboard bridge
• Recovering a classic game's sound-panning function
• An independent accounting app informed by QuickBooks research
• A video editor informed by CapCut research
• Making Axure prototype files scriptable

Each case links to the original work and explains what was demonstrated, what was reported, and what remains unverified.

Browse the collection: https://github.com/aliaihub/rea-casebook

Have a documented REA investigation? Contributions are welcome.

## Suggested launch sequence

1. Set the repository description, topics, and social-preview image.
2. Share the launch post from your own accounts with the preview image.
3. Publish one short walkthrough of a specific case, crediting its author and linking directly to its evidence.
4. Share in relevant community showcase channels where project submissions are welcome. Follow each community's rules.
5. Ask contributors for missing evidence or corrections and add substantive cases as they appear.

These are proposed actions; no social posts or outreach have been sent. Avoid claims of full-app reconstruction unless the evidence supports them.
