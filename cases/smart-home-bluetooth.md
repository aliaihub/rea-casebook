# Smart-home Bluetooth: a reported Mac controller

Category: Hardware

Evidence: **Author report** · Stage: **Working implementation**

Last source review: 2026-10-14

## Target and question

Control a personally owned smart toilet from a desktop menu-bar application.

## REA workflow and result

The discovery collection attributes to thinkszyg a REA/Codex investigation of a mini-program’s Bluetooth communication and a reported lid-control implementation, later extended to other home devices.

## Evidence and limits

This is a reported result, not an independently reproduced hardware integration. Device models, complete protocols, code, and cross-device tests are not established here. Original posts were not independently inspected.
